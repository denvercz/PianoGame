LENGTHS AFTER ":"

unit: beat (default): :1 is one beat (a quarter note), :1/2 an eighth.
unit: whole: lengths are note values, :1/4 quarter, :1/8 eighth, :1/2 half.
unit: bar: :1/12 is a twelfth of the bar.
Leave it out and the game picks the one that makes your bars add up.

    HOW TO PLAY

    THE KEYS

    Seven lanes, one for each note: C D E F G A B. Press the lane's key when a tile reaches the rainbow line. You can change the keys in Settings.

    Sharps and flats sit on their letter's lane (F♯ on F, B♭ on B). The tile shows a small # or b, and the game plays the right sound.

    TIMING

    Wrong keys don't cost points. Slow songs down to 50–75% on the song screen while you learn. Change how strict the timing is in Settings.

    MODES

    PART: play the TREBLE CLEF (right hand, top staff), the BASS CLEF (left hand, bottom staff), or BOTH. Whatever you don't play is played for you, note for note from the score.

    Turn on Show advanced parts in Settings to pick layers instead: MELODY, ACCOMP. and BASS, any mix of them.

    PLAY is scored. PRACTICE stops at each note until you press it. WATCH plays the song for you.

    PIANO view shows keys at the bottom. LIGHTS view shows only lanes that light up as tiles come.

    YOUR OWN SONGS

    Put typed .txt sheets in the Inputs folder (inside public) and they appear in the song list. UPLOAD SHEET saves a typed sheet there for you (Chrome or Edge), and also reads MIDI and MusicXML files. A photo or PDF is shown beside the editor so you can type the notes in while you look at it.

    In a sheet, RH:/RHA: lines are the treble clef, LH:/LHA:/LHM: lines are the bass clef. Fix anything in SHEET (text) or ROLL (grid), and save.
